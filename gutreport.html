GutReport

A mobile app and clinician dashboard that keeps young IBD patients connected to their doctors between check-ins. Patients track symptoms and medications; doctors monitor remotely and see who needs attention. Built with UC Irvine and CHOC, and shaped by feedback from the kids who use it.
